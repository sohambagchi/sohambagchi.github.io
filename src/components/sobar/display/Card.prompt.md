Container for a project, essay, or fact; use for grids of related things.

```jsx
<Card eyebrow="Essay" index="№ 07" title="On keeping things" meta="March 2026 · 9 min" href="#">
  Why I still write letters, and what they remember that I don't.
</Card>
<Card variant="inverse" title="Currently">Reading old maps.</Card>
```

- Variants: `surface` (default paper), `outline`, `inverse` (ink), `accent` (cinnabar — sparingly).
- Body copy is serif; title is display; meta is mono.
